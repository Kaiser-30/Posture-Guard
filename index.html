ESP32 Hardware & Connection Guide

This dashboard reads the exact USB Serial stream produced by your ESP32 sketch at 115200 baud — no extra Wi-Fi or firmware changes required.

Component ESP32 Pin Notes
MPU6050 SDA GPIO 21 I²C data line
MPU6050 SCL GPIO 22 I²C clock line
Haptic Motor GPIO 12 Latches HIGH after 5s > 10°
Calibrate Button GPIO 4 INPUT_PULLUP (other leg to GND)

Quick Connection Checklist

  1. Upload posture_monitor.ino to your ESP32.
  2. Close the Arduino IDE Serial Monitor (only one app can access the COM port at once).
  3. Open this page in Chrome or Edge and click Connect ESP32.
  4. Select your ESP32 USB-to-Serial port (e.g. CP210x or CH340).
